When this skill is invoked, produce complete landing page copy optimized for conversion. A landing page's job is one thing: get the visitor to take one specific action. Every element of copy serves or undermines that goal.

## Before Writing

Clarify:
1. **What is the offer?** Product, free trial, demo, download, event registration, or something else?
2. **Who is the audience?** Job title, company type, awareness stage (cold traffic vs. warm retargeting)?
3. **What is the primary CTA?** One action only. "Sign up" and "Learn more" on the same page split conversion.
4. **What is the strongest proof?** Customer logos, testimonials, case study stats, awards?
5. **What are the top objections?** What makes a qualified visitor hesitate or leave?
6. **What is the brand voice?** Formal/casual, technical/accessible, confident/humble?

## Landing Page Structure

```markdown
# Landing Page Copy: [Product/Offer Name]

---

## ABOVE THE FOLD

### Headline
[The single most compelling statement of the value this product/offer delivers.
Formula: [Outcome] for [Audience] without [obstacle] / in [timeframe].
Keep under 10 words. Must pass the "so what?" test.]

**Headline option A**: [...]
**Headline option B**: [Different angle — benefit vs. problem vs. question]

### Subheadline
[1–2 sentences expanding on the headline. Add specificity. Name who this is for.]

### Hero CTA
**Button text**: [Action verb + what they get. "Start free trial" not "Submit."]
**Supporting micro-copy below button**: [Remove friction. E.g., "No credit card required • Cancel anytime"]

---

## SOCIAL PROOF BAR
[Logo strip: "Trusted by [X companies] including [Name], [Name], [Name]"
OR "[X] teams use [Product] to [outcome]"]

---

## PROBLEM / PAIN SECTION
**Headline**: [Name the pain the audience knows they have]

[2–4 sentences or bullets describing the problem in the audience's own language.
Mirror the words they use to describe the problem — not internal product language.]

---

## SOLUTION SECTION
**Headline**: [Position the product as the relief]

[3 feature/benefit blocks. For each:]
- **Feature name**: [What it does]
- **Benefit**: [What the user gains or stops suffering from]
- **Proof point**: [Stat, quote, or example if available]

---

## SOCIAL PROOF SECTION
**Testimonial 1**:
"[Quote that addresses the top objection or names a specific result]"
— [Name, Title, Company]

**Testimonial 2**:
[Different angle — different use case or audience segment]

**Case study stat**: [Specific, credible number. Not "improved productivity" — "reduced report prep from 4 hours to 30 minutes."]

---

## OBJECTION HANDLING / FAQ
[2–4 questions that represent genuine hesitations. Answer honestly and directly.]

**Q**: [Most common objection phrased as a question]
**A**: [Direct answer — don't dodge it]

---

## FINAL CTA SECTION
**Headline**: [Low-friction invitation to act. "Start in 5 minutes." "See it in action." "Join [X] teams."]

**CTA button**: [Same or escalated version of hero CTA]
**Micro-copy**: [Repeat the friction-remover: "Free 14-day trial • No credit card required"]

---

## SEO / META
**Page title**: [Under 60 characters]
**Meta description**: [Under 155 characters — should include primary CTA]
```

## Copywriting Principles for Conversions

**Specificity builds credibility**: "Saves time" is ignored. "Teams save 4 hours per week on status reporting" creates belief.

**Features tell, benefits sell**: Lead with the outcome, follow with how it works.

**Objections not addressed become exit reasons**: Every qualified visitor has a reason not to convert. Address the top 3 before they bounce.

**The first line is the most important**: If the headline doesn't immediately resonate, nothing below matters.

**One page, one goal**: A landing page with multiple CTAs (demo + trial + download + newsletter) converts lower than one with a single clear action.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Product-centric copy**: "We built a platform that..." — start with the customer's problem, not your product.

**Jargon as positioning**: "AI-powered synergistic workflow automation" says nothing. Name what the user actually gets.

**Testimonials without specifics**: "Great product! 5 stars" has no conversion impact. "Cut our sprint planning from 3 hours to 45 minutes" does.

**Missing friction-removers**: Risk anxiety (price, commitment, learning curve) kills conversion. Name and remove the objections in micro-copy near the CTA.
