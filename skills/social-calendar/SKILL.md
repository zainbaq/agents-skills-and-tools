When this skill is invoked, produce a complete 30-day social media content calendar. A calendar is not a list of post ideas — it is a strategic framework that maintains brand voice, content variety, and platform-appropriate formats throughout the month.

## Before Building

Gather or clarify:
1. **Brand/person**: What is the brand or person this calendar is for? Industry, personality, tone?
2. **Platforms**: Which platforms? (LinkedIn, Instagram, X, TikTok, Threads, Facebook)
3. **Content pillars**: 3–5 recurring themes. Examples: thought leadership, behind-the-scenes, product education, social proof, community/engagement.
4. **Posting frequency**: How many posts per week per platform?
5. **Goals**: Awareness, community building, lead gen, or retention?
6. **Any upcoming events or launches**: Product releases, events, holidays, or campaigns to incorporate.

## Content Calendar Principles

**Pillar distribution matters**: If every post is thought leadership, the account feels like a lecture. Aim for balanced pillar distribution across the month.

**Format variety within platforms**: A 30-day LinkedIn calendar should mix text posts, carousels, polls, documents, and short videos — not 30 identical text posts.

**Platform-native formats**:
- LinkedIn: text (with line breaks), carousels, documents, polls, newsletters
- Instagram: carousels, Reels, Stories, static images, collaborative posts
- X: threads, single tweets, quote posts, image posts
- TikTok: short-form video (15s–3min), duets, trends
- Threads: conversational text, questions, short takes

**Mix engagement intent**: Not every post should ask for a like or share. Mix awareness posts (no CTA), engagement posts (comment/share prompt), and conversion posts (click CTA) roughly 60/30/10.

## Quality Bar

A good content calendar:
- Has distinct, platform-native captions (not the same caption across platforms)
- Balances all 5 content pillars — no pillar dominates the month
- Varies formats and engagement intent throughout
- Has specific, post-appropriate CTAs (not all "visit our website")
- Feels coherent as a month — there's a narrative thread, not just random posts

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Same caption on every platform**: LinkedIn, Instagram, and X require fundamentally different writing styles. Each platform entry must be written natively.

**Every post is a promotion**: A feed that only promotes products exhausts followers. 80% of posts should provide value before asking for anything.

**Generic CTAs**: "Like and follow for more!" is not a CTA. "Drop your answer below," "Save this for your next planning session," and "Tag the person who needs to hear this" are CTAs.

**Vague post topics**: "Monday motivation post" is not a calendar entry. Write the actual caption concept.
