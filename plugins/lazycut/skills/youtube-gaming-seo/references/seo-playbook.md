# SEO Playbook (reference for youtube-gaming-seo)

Channel facts are placeholders in angle brackets (`<Channel name>`, `<channel tagline>`, the subscribe link). Fill them from the channel brief the caller points to; see `channel-brief.example.md` in this folder.

These are the stable conventions. The game facts change every time and must be researched fresh; these patterns don't. Read this when you're at Step 3 (building the metadata).

## Title-pattern library

Front-load the exact search phrase, then add the hook. Aim to keep the core phrase inside the first ~60 characters so it survives truncation. YouTube allows 100 chars but search/mobile surfaces cut off earlier.

Produce four titles per video:

**1. Recommended** — search phrase + a clean difficulty/skill hook that matches reality.
```
<Game> - <Subject> Boss Fight (Flawless)
<Game> - <Subject> FINAL BOSS Fight (Flawless)   ← if it's the final boss, say so; "final boss" is a high-volume search
```

**2. Search-max** — pack the phrasing people actually type; good when you'd rather maximize discoverability than punch.
```
<Game> <Subject> Boss Fight | <Questline/Arc or "No Damage">
```

**3. No-hit / No-damage variant** — the highest-CTR option, USE ONLY IF TRUE.
```
<Game> - <Subject> | No Hit Boss Fight
<Game> - <Subject> Boss Fight (No Damage)
```

**4. On-brand variant** — leans on the channel tagline from the brief; softer, personality-forward. For a tagline about making hard games look easy:
```
How to Beat <Subject> - <Game> Boss Made Easy
```

Notes:
- Use the real "final boss" / "secret boss" / "optional boss" descriptor when accurate — each is its own search term.
- If the game has a well-known related predecessor, you can occasionally reference it, but don't clutter the title; save that for tags.
- Never put a difficulty claim in the title that the run doesn't back up.

## Description template

The first 1–2 lines are the most valuable real estate — they show in search results and feed the algorithm's understanding of the video. Put the subject name, the game, and the hook there. Then keep it ~150+ words with natural keywords (not stuffed).

```
<Subject> — <one-line who/what they are and why the fight matters> Here's the full fight, clean.

<2–4 sentences of accurate specifics: phases, the signature mechanic, the weapon/counter that matters, the questline it's in. This is where you prove you actually beat it.>

<Channel name> — <channel tagline>

▶ <Game> — All Boss Fights playlist: [paste your playlist link]
▶ Subscribe: <subscribe link from the channel brief>

CHAPTERS
0:00 Intro
0:00 <Phase / segment>
0:00 Kill

Boss: <Real Name> (<nickname / arc>)
Game: <Game> (<platform>, <studio>)

#<GameHashtag> #<SubjectHashtag> #BossFight
```

Rules that matter:
- The first CHAPTERS entry must be `0:00` or YouTube won't render chapters at all. Leave the times as `0:00` placeholders and tell the user to fill in their real ones.
- Three chapters minimum for chapters to work; each must be ≥10 seconds apart in reality.
- Exactly 3 hashtags is the sweet spot — the first three show above the title; more than ~3 and YouTube may ignore them.
- Keep the playlist link as a placeholder token the user pastes; don't fabricate a URL.

## Tag-construction recipe

Target ~18–20 tags, comma-separated, most-important first. Build from these buckets:

1. **Game name** and close variants — `<game>`, `<game> boss fight`, `<game> bosses`, `<game> <platform>`.
2. **Subject, both names** — the searched nickname (`the dragon boss fight`) AND the real name (`ukichi saito`). This is the superfan gap most channels miss.
3. **Difficulty terms, honest** — `no damage`, `no hit boss fight`, `flawless` — only if true.
4. **Accurate genre + mechanic** — e.g. `samurai game`, `katana`, `parry`, `counter`. NOT a false genre. For a real Soulslike, `soulslike` / `souls` are correct; for a samurai action game they are not.
5. **Arc / questline / area** if it's a searchable term (`<game> ending`, `<questline name>`).
6. **Related-game piggyback** — one or two, e.g. `ghost of tsushima sequel`, only when genuinely relevant.
7. **Channel + protagonist** — the channel name from the brief, and the playable character's name.

Don't keyword-stuff or repeat near-identical tags; YouTube weights the first several most.

## Thumbnail guidance

The channel owner usually already has thumbnails but wants them dialed in. Give this checklist plus a suggested word per video.

- **Don't repeat the title text.** Title says what; thumbnail says *why to click*. Put a result/reaction word: `FLAWLESS`, `NO HIT`, `0 DAMAGE`, `FINAL BOSS`.
- **2–4 words max**, huge and bold — must be legible as a tiny image on a phone (most viewers are on mobile).
- **High contrast, bright accent** (red reads well against most dark game palettes), with a subtle outline/glow separating the subject from the background.
- **Consistent template across the set** — same font, same text corner, same little channel mark. A recognizable series lifts CTR on every video once one hits.
- **Rank by drama** — give the final boss / most-searched fight the most intense thumbnail.
- Native A/B thumbnail testing unlocks at 1,000+ subs; note it as a future tool, not available yet on a small channel.

## Pre-publish checklist (include at the bottom of every output)

1. Real timestamps in every CHAPTERS block (first one MUST be `0:00`).
2. Category set to **Gaming**.
3. Playlist link pasted into all descriptions; add every video to the playlist.
4. End screen pointing to the *next* boss fight (keeps them in the loop → session watch time).
5. Pin a question comment (e.g. "Which fight was hardest for you?") to seed early engagement.
6. Turn on auto-captions — they help search indexing.
7. Publish the most-searched video (often the final boss) at a time you can reply to early comments; first-hour engagement velocity matters most.

## Worked example (Ghost of Yotei — Lord Saito, the final boss)

Shows the target quality bar. Note the accurate real name, the phase specifics, honest difficulty variant, and clean per-field blocks.

```
Ghost of Yotei - Lord Saito FINAL BOSS Fight (Flawless)
```
**Alternates**
```
Ghost of Yotei The Final Hunt | Lord Saito Boss Fight (No Damage)
Ghost of Yotei - Lord Saito FINAL BOSS | No Hit
How to Beat Lord Saito - Ghost of Yotei Final Boss Made Easy
```
